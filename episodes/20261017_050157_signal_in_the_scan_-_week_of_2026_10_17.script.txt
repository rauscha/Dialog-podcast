CASPAR [even]: Women with extremely dense breasts already enrolled in the Dutch national mammography program, randomized to CEM or abbreviated MRI as their supplemental screen. CASPAR [even]: The Dutch population. Screening interval, density distribution, radiologist training in the Netherlands — none of that is universal. A system with different density prevalence and different workflow norms cannot import these numbers without asking whether the denominator matches.

CASPAR [direct]: CEM held up.
